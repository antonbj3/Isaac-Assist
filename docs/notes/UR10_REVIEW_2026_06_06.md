## 1d. CUP-FRAME / gripper mount — DEEP ROOT (Anton's GUI catch) + exact diagnosis + fix-path
**This is the most important architectural finding tonight. Anton's "why 90° to the side" was a FUNCTION root, not
just cosmetics.**

**What it is:** the asset cup is mounted via a runtime −110° "cup-align" hack (`_sg_cup_align_deg`, pick_place.py
~6428). It leaves the cup TILTED ~70° from straight-down. Consequences (both observed):
1. **Angled / "telepathic" picks** — the cup reaches the cube FROM THE SIDE (lateral), not top-down. The whole pick
   approach is architected around this lateral cup.
2. **Drop-fling** — the held cube hangs BESIDE the wrist (large radius from the wrist axis), so the transit IK
   reconfiguration FLINGS it in a big arc (CP-83: cube whipped to z≈1.6, released in air, lands outside the bin).
   A straight-down cup would hang the cube directly below → smaller swing radius → less fling.

**Why the "obvious" fix fails (4 correct-observation experiments):** setting the geometrically-correct −90° makes
the cup point straight down BUT the pick then FAILS — the cup descends to [-0.561,0.391,1.050] vs Cube_1 at
[-0.5,0.4,0.975] = 6 cm beside + 7.5 cm above the cube; the straight-down grip ray MISSES the 5 cm cube; the SG
later grips the bin's `BaseCube` instead. Raising/lowering the descend (descend_extra) doesn't fix it — the cup is
laterally OFF the cube, not just at the wrong depth. The −110° tilt is LOAD-BEARING: it's what reaches the cube.

**The real fix (gated, safe — default stays −110 so passers are untouched):** −90° mount **+** an ITERATIVE
FK DESCEND-CORRECTION in `_build_segments` for the pick descend (S2): plan → FK the solved joints → compute the
live cup world pos (= tool0 ⊗ measured cup-in-tool0 offset) → re-plan with the goal shifted so the cup TIP lands
on the cube top, directly above. 1–2 iters converge. This positions the now-straight-down cup over the cube →
clean top-down grip → cube hangs straight → **likely also reduces the CP-83/81 drop-fling** (the coaxial hang is
the lever the transit-swing fixes could not reach). This is a pick-approach RE-ARCHITECTURE — substantial but
fully gated to a new flag (`_ur10_cupframe_down`, default OFF) so the −110 default + the 11 passers are byte-identical.

**Decision for you:** invest in the gated −90 + FK-correction pick re-architecture (could fix pick-quality AND the
drop-fling cluster-wide), or keep −110 (functional-but-tilted) and treat the drop separately? I am implementing it
GATED (default OFF) so it's safe to evaluate; the default is NOT changed without your go.
